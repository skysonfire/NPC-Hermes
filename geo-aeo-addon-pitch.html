01

The pitch in one line

Why this belongs in the NPC Protocol offer stack now.

The unit of competition changed from a RANK (the blue link) to a CITATION — being named inside the AI's answer. SEO gets a business on stage; GEO/AEO makes them the script the speaker reads. NPC Protocol already sells "full AI presence" (site + voice agent). GEO/AEO is the missing third leg: we get you recommended in ChatGPT / Perplexity / Google AI Overviews.

Why it fits NPC specifically

The SEO/AEO agencies don't offer the phone agent, and the voice agencies don't do AI visibility. Bundling site + voice + GEO under one vendor is a moat the point solutions can't match. It also converts our existing website-build clients into recurring GEO retainers — the highest-leverage upsell we have.

Why now (dated proof)

  • ›Ahrefs: AI traffic is tiny but ~23× conversion on that slice.
  • ›HubSpot: buyers found via AI ~3× likelier to convert.
  • ›Search Atlas: ~5.5× on the AI slice.
  • ›Baseline: still ~1% of all web traffic — don't oversell volume.

The urgency hook (killer sales move)

  • ›Competitive benchmark: show the client their AI visibility side-by-side with a named rival.
  • ›"Your competitor is already recommended in ChatGPT. You aren't."
  • ›Concrete, undeniable, and it makes the audit an easy yes.

How to deliver (productized ops playbook)

Same "idiot-proof" pattern as the voice agent: one proven pipeline + one config per client. Theming, not custom work.
1

Baseline audit (paid, the closer)

Map the client's current AI visibility across the 5 primary engines on their key "best [trade] near me" prompts. Crawl for robots.txt / AI-crawler blocks, schema, answer front-loading, GBP + review health.

Deliverable: "Is your business invisible to AI?" report + competitive benchmark
2

Technical unlock (one-time, heavy)

Unblock AI crawlers (robots.txt), add/fix schema, front-load a 2–4 sentence direct answer on key pages, question-form H2s, add lists + tables, fix NAP consistency, submit to Bing index.

Deliverable: technical checklist signed off + Bing/AI-crawler access confirmed
3

Brand-mention engine (the #1 lever, recurring)

Brand mentions out-rank backlinks for AI citation. Build the pipeline: Reddit threads, Quora/forums, press releases (via Google News), guest posts, Wikipedia/Common Crawl presence.

Deliverable: 2–3 live brand placements / month
4

Listicle placement (recurring)

LLMs pull heavily from "top 10 / best X" articles. Get the client top-3 in their OWN listicles AND in others' (directories, niche sites, associations).

Deliverable: listicle placements + rankings tracked
5

Local entity authority (ICP-specific)

Google Business Profile optimization, review volume/sentiment/recency, local listicles, trade-association + BBB + local-media entity signals.

Deliverable: entity graph + review velocity
6

Monitor + report (the proof, monthly)

Track named-citation instances (not "rank") per engine, before/after vs the benchmark. This is the artifact that justifies the retainer and drives renewals.

Deliverable: monthly AI-visibility report
One GEO config per client (the voice-config pattern)

Business name · key services · top 10 "best [trade]" prompts · target engines · booking/GBP links · FAQ/knowledge base · review source · transfer/report cadence. New client = fill the config + run the pipeline, not build a new strategy. This is what protects margin.

These are real. The pitch only holds if we under-promise on volume and over-deliver on process + proof.
High · delivery

Client pays 60–90 days, asks "are we in ChatGPT yet?", answer is no. Churn + refund pressure + reputation hit. See deep-dive below.

Mitigate: scope the promise to the RAG layer; sell process + reporting, never a guaranteed citation.
High · market

It's ~1% of traffic

The honest baseline. If we oversell volume, one bad month exposes the gap and burns the relationship.

Mitigate: anchor on intent + trajectory + named-citation, not share-of-voice.
Medium · measurement

Attribution is fuzzy

No clean KPI, engines are non-deterministic, no API. Hard to prove "we did this."

Mitigate: report named-citation instances before/after; keep the competitive benchmark as the fixed reference.
Medium · dependency

Engine opacity + ToS

We depend on Bing/Google indexes and each engine's crawler policy. An update can reset visibility overnight.

Mitigate: diversify across 5 engines + owned mentions (the durable asset), don't bet on one.
Medium · positioning

"Just SEO with a new name"

If we don't name the different levers (Bing/RAG, mentions, listicles), the prospect won't feel it's different from SEO.

Mitigate: always name the levers; lead with the rank→citation reframe.
Low · cost

Margin erosion

Outsourcing too many placements/content drags gross margin from ~78% to ~40%.

Mitigate: default to lean in-house delivery; outsource only brand placements.
07

Risk of delivery failure — deep dive

The single biggest commercial risk, and exactly how we de-risk it before selling a single retainer.

⚠ What "failure" actually looks like

A client pays for 60–90 days of retainer, opens the monthly report, sees no (or marginal) lift in named-citation instances across the primary engines, and asks: "So what did we get?" That moment — not a bad month — is the delivery-failure event. It triggers churn, refund pressure, and a reputation hit that radiates into the site + voice offers.

Root drivers
  • Base-model lag: Claude/GPT base corpus takes 6–12 mo — can't be forced.
  • Engine opacity: no API, no guaranteed placement, non-deterministic ranking.
  • Low query volume: a niche local business may have too few AI prompts for any business to surface.
  • Model updates: one engine release can reset visibility overnight.
  • Attribution: hard to prove causation to a skeptical owner.
De-risking moves (before we sell)
  • Sell the audit first — it's paid, genuinely valuable, and produces the "before."
  • Scope the promise to the RAG layer (Bing+Google → 5 engines) — the only layer with a 60–90 day window.
  • Set a 60–90 day expectation; report named-citation instances, not "rank."
  • Guarantee process + reporting, never a citation — in writing.
  • Phase / milestone pricing, not full prepay — aligns risk with delivery.
  • Client selection filter (below) — only sell where the channel demonstrably works.
Client-selection filter — do NOT sell the retainer if…
  • Zero AI query volume in their niche (nobody gets cited, client or competitor).
  • No named local competitor is already cited (no proof the channel works for them).
  • They can't state a single concrete business outcome they want from AI visibility.
  • …in any of those cases, sell the audit + technical fixes only (one-time), not the recurring retainer.
