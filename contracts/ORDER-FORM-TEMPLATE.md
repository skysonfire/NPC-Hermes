### 2.1 AI Visibility Audit — one-time
`{{INCLUDE_AUDIT}}`

A fixed-scope diagnostic of the Client's visibility in answer engines.

**In scope**
- Citation checks across five engines: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Copilot,
  and Google AI Overviews.
- Each prompt run three times per engine in a fresh session; the majority
  result is reported.
- A named comparison against {{COMPETITOR_COUNT}} competitors agreed at intake.
- Technical crawl: AI-crawler access, structured data coverage, index presence.
- Entity checks: name, address and phone consistency across public directories.
- A prioritised fix list and a 60–90 day roadmap.
- Every finding evidenced with a timestamped screenshot and the raw log line.

**Out of scope** — delivery of any fix, content production, placement or
outreach work, base-model corpus work, and anything outside the agreed prompt
set and engine list. Those are the subject of section 2.4 or a separate Order
Form.

**Explicitly not promised.** The Provider does not guarantee a citation,
appearance, ranking, or position in any engine. These are third-party systems
outside the Provider's control. The Provider guarantees the process and the
data: the prompts stated, run the number of times stated, with evidence
attached.

### 2.3 Missed-Call Callback — recurring
`{{INCLUDE_CALLBACK}}`

An automated SMS to callers whose call to the Client's number goes unanswered.

**How it works.** The Client sets conditional call forwarding on no-answer to a
number supplied by the Provider. Calls the Client answers are unaffected and
are never seen by the Provider. Unanswered calls trigger a single text to the
caller identifying the Client and inviting a reply.

**The Client acknowledges and agrees that:**
- The text is sent from a number supplied by the Provider, not the Client's own
  number, and the Client's own number is quoted in the message body.
- Recipients who reply STOP are suppressed permanently and will not be texted
  again, and this suppression cannot be reversed by the Client.
- **The Provider warrants** that the message is a response to a call the
  recipient placed and will carry no offer, promotion, discount or other
  marketing content, and that it will identify the Client and provide an
  opt-out. The Provider authors and sends the message; this warranty sits with
  the Provider accordingly. The Client will not request any change that would
  breach it, and the Provider may refuse any such request.
- The agreed message wording is set out at section 8. Any change to it requires
  the written agreement of both parties.
- No text is sent outside {{QUIET_HOURS}} local time.
- The Client's own network or tariff may charge for the forwarded leg of a
  call. Those charges are the Client's.
- **The Client is the data controller** for the personal data of its callers.
  The Provider acts as processor under the Data Processing Addendum at
  {{SITE_URL}}/dpa, which is incorporated into this Order Form.

**Fee:** {{CALLBACK_FEE}} per month.
**Message allowance:** {{MESSAGE_ALLOWANCE}} texts per month; additional texts
at {{OVERAGE_RATE}} each.
